# Paper Presentation Series

The historical Big Presentation series ends at:

`versions/v88-big-presentation-hybrid-buttons/`

The journal-paper-aligned series starts separately:

| Version | Derived from | Scientific focus |
|---|---|---|
| `v01-paper-presentation-hybrid-buttons` | `v88-big-presentation-hybrid-buttons` | Calibration versus scene switching, matched-static attribution, cue audit, U2MOT rescore and SparseTrack supporting evidence |

Future versions in this family should use `v02-paper-presentation-*`,
`v03-paper-presentation-*`, and so on. They must not overwrite `v88` or reuse
the historical Big Presentation numbering.
